Questions for the Business Advisor

Battle Room · solo founder · Idaho LLC · B2B software subscription (SaaS)
Lead with these three — most likely to bite you and hardest to fix later:  1) Sales tax on SaaS (Idaho + other states)  ·  2) Quarterly estimated taxes  ·  3) Keeping the LLC liability shield intact.
Then ask: "If I could only do three things before launch, what would they be?"

Entity & Formation

  • Single-member LLC now vs. S-corp election later — at what revenue does S-corp start to save me money?
  • Do I need a registered agent — me or a service?
  • Idaho filings, fees & annual report requirements to stay in good standing?
  • Do I need a state or Canyon County / city business license to run a SaaS?
  • Should I get an EIN even as single-member? (Why / why not.)

Banking & Liability Shield

  • What do I need to open a business bank account?
  • What specifically puts my personal assets at risk ("piercing the veil") — and how do I avoid it?
  • How should I pay myself — owner's draw vs. salary?

Taxes (priority)

  • How is a single-member LLC taxed — what will I owe (federal, Idaho, self-employment)?
  • Do I owe quarterly estimated taxes, and how do I calculate them?
  • Sales tax on my subscription — taxable in Idaho? What about out-of-state customers ("economic nexus")?
  • What expenses can I deduct (hosting, software, insurance, home office, legal)?
  • Accounting software from day one — which?

Contracts & Legal

  • Should a lawyer review my Terms of Service & Privacy Policy before real customers — rough cost?
  • I collect user data + process payments (Stripe) — what privacy/data obligations am I missing (CA + other states)?
  • My subscriptions auto-renew — what auto-renewal disclosure laws must I follow?
  • Is click-to-agree on my Terms enough, or do customers need to sign something?

Insurance & Risk

  • What insurance does a SaaS actually need — Tech E&O, Cyber liability? Realistic cost?
  • Get it before my first paying customer, or at some threshold?

Payments & Records

  • Stripe specifics — tax reporting (1099-K), chargebacks, refund handling?
  • What records must I keep, and for how long?
Always ask: "For a solo founder launching a B2B software subscription in Idaho, what do people in my position most commonly get wrong or forget?"   |   Notes: jot their answers next to each box — flag anything that surprises you to follow up on.
